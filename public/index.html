The new web, as it appears

People, often working with AI coding tools, are publishing small websites and web apps faster than ever, and many disappear within months. This explorer finds them as they are announced on Hacker News, Bluesky and GitHub, takes a picture of each one, saves it in the Wayback Machine, and checks back for 90 days to see whether it is still there.
